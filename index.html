Bolsillo

Envelope budgeting that stays on your device · by Lusol Studio

Bolsillo is a personal budgeting app built on the envelope method: every unit of money you have gets a job before you spend it. It runs entirely offline — no account, no servers, no tracking.

Common questions

Where is my data? In a database inside the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded.

How do I move to a new phone? Settings → Data → Backup writes a full JSON file. Save it somewhere you control, then restore it on the new device.

Can you recover my budget if I lose my phone? No. We never receive a copy of your data, so a backup you made yourself is the only way back.
